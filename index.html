What do I need to rent a car?+

A valid driver’s license, a payment card, and a little sense of adventure. Age and eligibility requirements may vary by vehicle and location, so check your booking details before you go.

Can I change or cancel my booking?+

Plans can change. Your booking confirmation includes the change and cancellation terms for your reservation. Reach out to our team if you need a hand.

Do you offer weekly or monthly rentals?+

Yes. We offer long term rental options for gig workers by the week or month. Insurance and maintenance are included in the rental plan. Contact us for availability and details.

Where can I find your cars?+

We rent through platforms such as Turo and Facebook. Contact us if you need help finding an available vehicle.
